What changed from your list

  • Swap

    Better Sweater → Rab Nexus Hoody. The Better Sweater is a 638 g sweater-knit. It's slow to dry, sweaty on climbs and pills under pack straps. The Nexus weighs 308 g, breathes and stretches, and costs £65–80 less. The Better Sweater is under Later if you still want it for the pub.

  • Swap

    Torque Pants → Torque Mountain Pants. Regular fit, a part-elasticated waist and a boot gusset at the hem. The plain Torque is a slim climbing cut.

  • Cheaper

    Base layer: the same Icebreaker top is £67.95 at Cotswold, against £89.99 direct.

  • Cheaper

    Shell: the same Torrentshell is £144 at The Mountain Factor. Your Smolder Blue is £180 at Patagonia unless Mountain Factor has it in L.

  • Added

    Talini for stops and night shoots, Downpour overtrousers for proper rain, a waterproof cap for your glasses and a merino beanie.

  • Skip

    Lower base layer and tee. Leggings would overheat you on the move, so they're under Later for winter night shoots. A tee can wait for spring; the long-sleeve covers October to April.
